How the confidence levels work

  • Scale: under 5 is a Pass, 5 to 6.9 a Lean, 7 and up a Play.
  • Total: three last-5 sums against the line: the two goalies' goals allowed, the two offenses' goals scored, and the two defenses' goals allowed. Their average is the projection; about one goal away from the line reads as a 9, a tenth of a goal is a Pass. All three on the same side counts in full; two of three counts less.
  • Moneyline: how lopsided the matchup is: each offense against the other defense (goals, shots on goal, power play vs penalty kill, shot attempts), categories won, and the goalie edge in recent save %. Favorites of -250 or shorter stay a Lean at most.
  • Puck line: the moneyline read minus 3: the better team has to win by two, so only a clear mismatch gets there.
  • Goalie table: each probable starter's goals allowed, saves, save %, shots against and win % per start, by season, road or home, last 10 and last 5; the rank is his last 5 among every goalie with 2+ starts.
  • Goalies: P = probable, C = confirmed. Starters usually confirm around 1 to 2 PM ET; a change can move the total a lot.
  • Hits and blocks are shown and ranked but don't count as better or worse.
